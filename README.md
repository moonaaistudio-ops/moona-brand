# Moona Studio · Brand

כל העיצוב של MOONA במקום אחד: צבעים, גופנים, לוגואים, כללי כתיבה, ואיך בונים בריף והצעת מחיר. כל בוט, מכל מחשב, יכול לקרוא את הדף הזה ולבנות באותו עיצוב.

**איך משתמשים:** כותבים לבוט: "תבנה לפי https://github.com/moonaaistudio-ops/moona-brand".

## For any AI assistant or developer

1. Read this file first. It is the brand book; follow it exactly.
2. In any HTML page, load the one stylesheet and paint the page on the void ground:
   ```html
   <link rel="stylesheet" href="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/moonaaistudio-ops/moona-brand@main/moona.css">
   ```
   It holds every token (`--moona-*`, `--font-*`, `--r-*`), the Hebrew swap, and every component class (`mn-*`, `mn-doc*`, `mn-quote*`).
   Where an external stylesheet is not allowed, copy `moona.css` into the page.
3. Start a client brief from `examples/brief.html`, a quote page from `examples/quote-web.html` and its PDF from `examples/quote.html`. Replace every `[placeholder]`.
4. Logos are in `logos/`. Use them as files; never redraw them.
5. `tokens.json` has the same values as data, with a usage note on each.

| File | What |
|---|---|
| `moona.css` | The whole design in one stylesheet |
| `tokens.json` | Every colour, type style, radius, shadow, tracking and leading value, with usage notes |
| `examples/brief.html` | A complete client brief page (Hebrew, RTL) |
| `examples/quote-web.html` | A complete web quote page, as sent to clients (Hebrew, RTL) |
| `examples/quote.html` | The two-page A4 PDF quote that goes with it |
| `logos/` | Mark (SVG, PNG 512) and lockup (SVG) |

## Brand book

Moona Studio makes brand films and ads born without a camera. The site, briefs, quotes and decks all share one look: a deep void ground, pale ice as the voice of the interface, and a single gold accent that acts. Use this system for anything that carries the Moona name, in English or Hebrew.

## Content fundamentals

Write like a studio that controls every frame. The technology is sold as control and care for the product, never as something that was "generated" and never as the cheaper option.

- Lead with what the client gets on screen. Approved lines to reuse as they are:
  - "Cinematic ads, born without a camera" / "פרסומות קולנועיות, שנולדו בלי מצלמה"
  - "A brand-film studio. No shoot day." / "סטודיו לסרטי מותג. בלי יום צילום."
  - "Your product, protected in every shot." / "המוצר שלכם, בדיוק כמו שהוא, בכל שוט."
  - Credit line: "בלי מצלמה. בימוי עד הפריים האחרון."
- The main CTA is "Send one product" / "שלחו מוצר אחד". The header and secondary CTA say "Let's talk". The promise behind it: a reply with a direction within two business days.
- First person plural ("we"), short sentences, often in pairs. Talk to the client as a peer: no closing questions like "does this speak to you?", nothing written from below.
- Never use: "AI video", "AI-generated", "generated by AI", "generative", "Made with AI", "built with AI", "viral", "10x", "cheaper than", "הפקת AI", "נוצר ב-AI", "יצירת וידאו", "ויראלי", "זול מצילום".
- Two Hebrew traps: "מבוים" reads as staged and fake, "מדומה" reads as imaginary. Say "בימוי" (the noun) and "בנינו את העולם" instead.
- No em dash or en dash anywhere, in either language. Split the sentence, or use a comma or a colon.
- Avoid the "not X. Y." pair; it reads as written by a machine. One plain sentence is better.
- Product names keep their own spelling and case. Numbers, durations and timestamps stay LTR inside Hebrew (`.mn-ltr`).
- No emoji in product UI or documents.

## Colour

- Put every page on `moona-void`. There is no light theme; a white page is not Moona.
- Raise panels, cards and media to `moona-void-2`. Use `moona-scrim` behind anything that sits over video.
- Text: headlines and primary copy in `moona-ice-hot`; long body copy in `moona-body-dim`; labels and secondary text in `moona-ice-dim`. All three read on `moona-void` and `moona-void-2`.
- `moona-gold` is the only colour that acts: the CTA, focus rings, eyebrows, progress, the current step. Never add a second accent next to it.
- Emphasis inside a headline is `moona-gold-hot`, applied by writing `<em>`.
- Hairlines are `moona-line`; inactive borders `moona-ice-faint`; gold borders `moona-gold-line`; gold tints `moona-gold-wash` and `moona-gold-wash-hot` on hover.
- `moona-danger` is the only status colour, for form errors and rejected files, always with words.
- `color-1` to `color-5` are the warm-gold ramp of the CTA border only. Do not use them elsewhere.
- Short aliases (`void`, `ice`, `gold`, ...) exist because the live site still uses them; new work uses the `moona-` names.

## Typography

- Headlines: `--moona-display` (Space Grotesk 600, tracking `moona-track-display`). Styles `display`, `question`, `title`, `quote`, `card`.
- Body: `--moona-sans` (Inter Tight). Styles `lead`, `body`, `body-sm`, line height `moona-lh-body` or `moona-lh-loose`.
- Every label, eyebrow, badge, button, timestamp and the wordmark: `--moona-mono` (SF Mono), uppercase, tracked (`label-lg`, `label`, `label-sm`; `moona-track-label`, `moona-track-cta`). Mono uppercase is what makes a label a label.
- Instrument Serif (`serif`) belongs to the restored production hero only. Do not use it for section headings.
- Live sizes are fluid: use `--moona-fs-display`, `--moona-fs-title` and the rest from the stylesheet. The px values in the type styles are their maximums.
- Hebrew (`html[lang="he"]`): `--moona-display` and `--moona-sans` become Assistant (`hebrew`), headlines go to weight 700 with tracking -.025em, labels drop their letter-spacing. Mono stays Latin in both languages. Set `lang` and `dir` on `<html>`; never mirror components by hand.

## Space, shape and depth

- One rounded family: `r-pill` for buttons, chips and round controls; `r-lg` for media, cards and tiles; `r-md` for panels and FileDrop; `r-sm` for menu rows.
- Nothing interactive is smaller than `moona-tap` (44px).
- Content width `moona-max` (1200px); the flagship film `moona-max-wide` (1560px); sides `--moona-gutter`. Sections breathe: `--moona-section-space`.
- Depth comes from long soft shadows on dark: `moona-shadow-panel` for cards, `moona-shadow-frame` for media, `moona-shadow-stage` for the hero film, `moona-shadow-menu` for menus, `moona-glow-gold` for a gold hover glow.

## Motion

- One easing everywhere: `--ease` (cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1)). Durations `--moona-dur-fast` .3s, `--moona-dur` .45s, `--moona-dur-slow` 1s, `--moona-dur-reveal` 1.05s.
- Media opens through the aperture (`Frame`); text rises once (`Reveal`). Once seen, stays seen.
- Every animation has a `prefers-reduced-motion` branch that shows the content at rest.
- Never animate a blur, and mount video only for the piece in view.

## Logos

- `logos/moona-logo-mark.svg`: the gold faceted aperture disc. Use it at 24 to 28px in the header with a soft gold drop shadow, and as an avatar or favicon.
- `logos/moona-logo-lockup.svg`: the mark with the MOONA STUDIO wordmark in SF Mono, ice `#dcecff`. Use it on `moona-void` only.
- `logos/moona-logo-mark-512.png`: raster mark for places that cannot take SVG.
- Do not redraw, recolour or place the logo on a light ground.
- On reels and free samples the only mark is a quiet `@moonastudio.ai` at the bottom right, there to stop copies, never as a brand display.

## Iconography

- Icons are simple line drawings: 1.4 to 1.5px stroke in `currentColor`, round caps, about 15 to 17px in a 16 or 18 grid (the mail, upload, close and play glyphs in the components).
- Icons take the colour of their control: `moona-gold` inside IconButton, FileDrop and MailButton.
- The CTA caret is drawn with two borders, not a glyph, so it points the same way in every font and flips under RTL.
- No icon font, no emoji.

## Components

Thirteen components, all drawn for the dark ground; the page root must paint `moona-void`. Class names (`mn-btn`, `mn-card`, ...) belong to the system; never author, extend or imitate them. Write your own layout in plain CSS that reads the tokens.

- Actions: `Button` (one per view), `MailButton`, `IconButton`, plus `RoundButton` inside the header.
- Navigation: `Header` with `Lockup`, `Nav`, `NavLink`, `RoundButton`.
- Layout: `Section`, `Frame`, `Card`.
- Content: `SectionHeading`, `Chip`, `Steps`, `Marquee`.
- Motion: `Reveal`. Inputs: `FileDrop`.

A typical block: a `Section` with a `SectionHeading` (eyebrow, title with one `<em>`, note), then three `Card`s in a grid, each inside a `Reveal` staggered by .12s.

## Documents: client briefs and quotes

Briefs and quotes are the benchmark for anything Moona sends a client. They share the site's colours and logo but read as documents: Hebrew first, softer, more air. Build them from the `mn-doc` and `mn-quote` classes in `moona.css`.

**Brief pages** (`DocHero`, `DocIdea`, `DocCards`, `DocEpisodes`, `DocClose`). Good examples: GOOM (goom/v1), ecoLove (ecolove/v1), Marina (marina/brief).
- `<html lang="he" dir="rtl">`, ground `moona-void`, side padding clamp(16px, 4vw, 56px), content max 1180px.
- Text is Assistant (`hebrew`): base `brief-body` 18px in `moona-ice-hot`; every secondary line `brief-muted`, weight 300 in `moona-ice-mute`. Titles 600, card titles 700. No Space Grotesk headlines in a brief.
- Eyebrows, numbers and the signature: `brief-label` mono uppercase, LTR, `moona-ice` or `moona-gold`. The eyebrows may be English ("The idea", "How we work").
- Sections are separated by a `moona-line-doc` hairline, padding clamp(56px, 8vw, 112px).
- Order: hero (client logo × MOONA lockup, eyebrow, title, sub-line, format line, 9:16 frame) → the idea (one quoted line, gold punchline, "למה זה.") → frame or sample → options or episodes (cards) → how we work (five stage cards, the current one `is-chosen`) → close (logos, one line, next step, signature **MOONA STUDIO** · TAL TZUR).
- Every brief lives at `moonastudio.ai/<client>/v<n>/`, `noindex`. A new version is a new URL; never overwrite a link a client has.
- One sample frame or film is enough; label free samples quietly at the top right (© MOONA / להמחשה בלבד).

**Web quote page** (`WebQuoteIncludes`, `WebQuotePrice`, `WebQuoteTerms`, `WebQuoteApprove`): the format sent to Marina on 07.10, the benchmark.
- Same page language as a brief, at `moonastudio.ai/<client>/quote-v<n>/`. Top: eyebrow "MOONA STUDIO × CLIENT" with the lockup (`mn-doc__top`), then the hero with title "<project>. הצעת מחיר.", a sub-line, the format line and a gold link to the brief.
- Order: what goes into an episode (numbered rows with English craft tags) → the price (tiers in a gold ledger) → how we work (the same rows, current step gold) → terms (bold-led paragraphs) → approval box (fields, checkbox, gold button, PDF link) → close with logos, signature and the legal line.
- Prices in Space Grotesk on the left, "+ מע״מ" under them. The tier the client starts with is gold.

**PDF quote** (`QuoteHeader`, `QuotePricing`, `QuoteProcess`, `QuoteTerms`)
- Attached to every quote email, and the file the web page's PDF link opens. A4 pages on `moona-void`, printed to PDF and DOCX from the quote template. Two pages: header, parties, opening, scope, pricing; then process, terms, not included, approval.
- Title "הצעת מחיר" in Space Grotesk 700 34px; everything else Assistant 11.5px; labels in 9px mono gold or ice.
- No quote numbers (no MS-2026-xxx) on the page, in the file name or in the email. Title and date only.
- Prices: a gold header row, tabular LTR amounts, the grand total in `moona-gold`. Describe the work each item needs; never argue for a discount.
- Send the PDF as an attachment. A link is extra, never the only copy.

## Not synced

From the site repo `moonaaistudio-ops/moona-studio-site`, folder `design-system/`. The site's React components are not shipped here; `moona.css` carries their real `mn-` classes, so plain HTML gets the same look.
